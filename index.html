A prevention and health-equity platform for underserved communities.

EDDIE, Eliminating Disparities through Data-driven Intelligent Empowerment, is a family of health education, prevention, and research-access tools developed under the clinical leadership of a board-certified surgical oncologist.

How the platform fits together

Learn

Ask EDDIE, Ask SHELIA, and the EDDIE Lung, Colon, and Prostate apps deliver plain-language cancer and women's health education.

Prevent

Eddie Prevention is the flagship behavior-change platform: daily and weekly check-ins and progress tracking for tobacco, alcohol, nutrition, and activity. CancerLess is its cancer-prevention program.

Access research

The Clinical Trials Navigator helps patients find trials; Clinical Trials Pro supports institutional research teams with trial screening.
